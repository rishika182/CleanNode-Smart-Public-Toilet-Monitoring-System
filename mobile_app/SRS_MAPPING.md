## 2. Non-Functional Requirements (NFR) Verification

* **NFR1 (Real-time Responsiveness):** Continuous background evaluation interval of 12 seconds for sensor changes and timer deadlines.
* **NFR2 (Graceful Degradation):** Full application operational capability with fallback models when offline or without a Gemini API key.
* **NFR3 (Privacy & Security):** Phone numbers masked where appropriate; unmonitored private restrooms explicitly protected against fabricated telemetry.
* **NFR4 (Accessibility & Multilingual Equality):** Complete parity across English, Kannada, and Hindi across all UI labels, alert notifications, and AI responses.
